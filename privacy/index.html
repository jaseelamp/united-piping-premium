Privacy policy pending approval

This page is not an approved legal privacy policy. The company must supply and approve legal content before a public launch or live enquiry collection.

Browser storage

The enquiry builder stores selected products and their technical requirements in localStorage on your device. Use “Clear enquiry” to remove them. Contact details and uploaded documents are not persisted by this website.

Enquiry drafts

With no backend configured, forms create a downloadable draft on your device. They do not transmit your enquiry to United Piping. Emailing a draft or following a telephone link uses your chosen service.

External resources

Core libraries and images are hosted with this website. External map and email links open third-party services only when selected. No analytics integration is included in this implementation.
